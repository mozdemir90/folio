# Folio — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 6, 2026  
**Effective Date:** November 6, 2026

At Folio ("we", "our", or "the App"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information when you use the Folio mobile application (Android) and desktop editions.

---

## 1. Core Principle: Offline and Privacy-First

Folio is designed from the ground up as a **local-first, offline document viewer**.

- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, device identifiers, or IP address.
- **No User Account Required:** You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to use Folio.
- **No Remote Servers:** Folio does not operate any backend servers, cloud databases, or remote file storage facilities.

---

## 2. Document and File Access

Folio functions exclusively to open, render, and display PDF documents that you explicitly select:

1. **Local-Only Processing:** All PDF documents you view are processed entirely in your device's local memory (RAM).
2. **Zero Remote Transmission:** Your documents, document text, annotations, and metadata are never uploaded to any remote server or shared with any third party.
3. **Scoped File Picker:** The application accesses files solely through standard system file picker interfaces (`file_picker` / Android Storage Access Framework) upon your explicit action. The app cannot and does not read any other files or photos without your direct selection.

---

## 3. Permissions Used

- **Storage / File Access:** Used strictly to allow you to browse and select PDF files on your device to open and render them.
- **No Internet Required:** The application does not require internet connectivity for its core functionality and does not transmit telemetry data over the network.

---

## 4. Third-Party Libraries and SDKs

The App contains **no advertising SDKs** (such as Google AdMob), **no analytics or behavioral tracking SDKs** (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), and **no third-party data broker services**.

The open-source libraries used (such as `pdfrx` / PDFium and `file_picker`) operate strictly locally on your device to decode and render PDF pages.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Folio does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). The application is safe for general audiences of all ages.

---

## 6. Data Security and Retention

Because Folio does not transmit or store any personal data or documents on external servers, there is no risk of cloud-based server breaches. When you uninstall Folio, any temporary application cache maintained by the operating system is automatically deleted.

---

## 7. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA & International Regulations)

Under applicable data protection laws, individuals have rights regarding their personal data. Because Folio does not collect, track, or retain any personal data on any server, there are no server records to inspect, modify, or erase.
